When to pay

Buy after the free check proves the study gap.

Good fit for $19

  • You have an exam date and need a four-week priority sprint.
  • Your weak domains are clear, but your review blocks are scattered.
  • You want printable recall prompts and a final 48-hour checklist.

Good fit for $49

  • You want a reviewer or mentor to inspect the plan quickly.
  • You need a cleaner handoff with questions, risks, and final-week edits.
  • You are deciding whether to reschedule or narrow the final review.

Skip payment when

  • You need official ISA material, eligibility decisions, or a pass guarantee.
  • You want recalled exam questions or a substitute for current safety training.
  • The free diagnostic already gives enough structure for your study week.
